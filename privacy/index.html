Contacts you add

If you ask AskGogo to remind someone else, you give us that person's name and phone number. We use it only to send the reminder you asked for, in your name. That person can reply STOP at any time and we will not message them again. Do not add someone who would not expect to hear from you.

How we use it

  • To run the service you asked for — setting reminders, keeping lists, answering questions
  • To send you the messages you have scheduled, and briefings you have switched on
  • To enforce the limits of your plan
  • To investigate faults, abuse and security incidents
  • To meet legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use the content of your messages to advertise to you.

Google user data

If you connect Google Calendar or Gmail, AskGogo's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use that data only to provide the features you asked for. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not let humans read it except with your explicit permission, to resolve a fault you have reported, or where the law requires it. You can disconnect Google at any time from your account settings or from your Google account permissions page.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active, because the service depends on it — a reminder you set last month has to still be there next month.

  • Reminders, lists and notes: kept until you delete them or close your account
  • Message content: kept for 12 months, then deleted
  • Error and usage logs: kept for 90 days
  • Payment records: retained as long as tax and accounting law requires

When you close your account we delete your personal data within 30 days, except anything we must keep by law.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and who we have shared it with
  • Ask us to correct or complete anything inaccurate
  • Ask us to erase your data, where we are not required to keep it
  • Withdraw consent you have given, at any time
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated
  • Complain to the Data Protection Board of India if we do not resolve your grievance

To exercise any of these, message AskGogo on WhatsApp or write to support@askgogo.in. We respond within 30 days.
