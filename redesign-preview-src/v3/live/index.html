More about us

Questions

Things people ask before they hand something over.

01What can Gogo actually do?+
Remember what you tell it and what you send it, set reminders that know the context, find your documents, compare options, book appointments and services, and follow up until the thing is done. It works through WhatsApp, your calendar, your mail and the web.
02Does it do things without asking me?+
Reading your own files, reminders and research happen on their own. Anything that spends money, books something or messages another person waits for your yes, in the chat, with the details in front of you.
03Where does my data live?+
In your own account. You can see everything Gogo remembers on the dashboard, correct it, or delete it. We do not sell personal data.
04Do I need to install an app?+
No. Gogo lives in WhatsApp. The web dashboard is there when you want the overview; you never have to use it.
05Which countries and currencies?+
India first, then the world. Searches, bookings and services are chosen for where you live, in your currency, on the sites you actually use.
06What does it cost?+
Private beta is free by invitation. Paid plans come later and will be announced to the waitlist first.
