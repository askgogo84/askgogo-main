How connections work

Permission first, every time.

You switch each app on

Nothing is connected by default. Gogo asks for one app at a time, when a task needs it.

Least access that works

Read-only where reading is enough. Payments and messages to others still wait for your yes.

Every task names its app

Done comes with which service was used, when, and the confirmation it returned.

Disconnect in one tap

Switch an app off and Gogo stops using it right away. Memories from it stay yours to keep or delete.
