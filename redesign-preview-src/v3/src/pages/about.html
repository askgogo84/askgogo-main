Why we are building this

Daily life in India runs on OTP logins, appointment portals, utility websites and long WhatsApp threads. That is a lot of small, repetitive work. An assistant that carries it, carefully and with your permission, gives people real time back.

AskGogo is in private beta and opens to new people in small groups as the product earns it.
