What it is

The memory is what your company knows. The agents are what gets done with it. Each answer and each action shows where it came from.

What it rests on

Four parts, each one checkable.

Shared company memory

Policies, past decisions, vendor and customer history in one permissioned memory. People and agents draw on the same source, and every answer shows where it came from.

Work carried through

Hand off recurring work end to end: chasing invoices, onboarding vendors, scheduling across teams, following up on open requests. Agents work through email, calendars and web portals.

Approvals

You decide which actions run on their own and which wait for a person. Payments, external messages and commitments go to the right approver with full context.

Verification

Each workflow ends with evidence: a confirmation, a reply, a changed record. Work that cannot be verified is flagged for a person instead of being marked complete.

Principles

How SecondBrain treats what your company knows.

Provenance

Every remembered fact carries its source: you said it, a document showed it, or a calendar confirmed it. Nothing guessed is presented as known.

Correction

You can see what is remembered and change it. When you remove a memory, it is removed, not hidden.

Scope

Company memory follows your roles and permissions. Personal memory in AskGogo stays personal. The two never mix by default.

Expiry

A flight ends, a gate code changes, an offer lapses. Facts are allowed to age out instead of being acted on after they stop being true.
