Pricing questions

Can I really use AskGogo for free?

Yes. The Free plan is designed to let you experience the actual product, not just a static demo.

Which plan should most people choose?

Lite is the easiest paid entry at ₹99. Pro at ₹299 is the main full-featured plan and is intended to be the best fit for regular AskGogo users.

Do I lose my memories if I downgrade?

Your saved memories remain yours. Usage limits affect how much new work Gogo can do, not whether your existing information disappears.
