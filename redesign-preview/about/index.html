About

Built in Bengaluru, for the way people here live.

We think the next useful step for AI is not a better answer. It is a finished task.

Daily life in India runs on OTP logins, appointment portals, utility websites and long WhatsApp threads. That is a lot of small, repetitive work. An assistant that carries it, carefully and with your permission, gives people real time back. AskGogo is in private beta and opens to new people in small groups as the product earns it.

Founder

Goverdhan Mandyam Dommane

Goverdhan has spent his career building consumer technology companies in India, including one that was acquired. He started AskGogo to build the assistant he wanted for himself: one that remembers, acts and finishes the job.

What we hold ourselves to

Finish, then report

We judge ourselves by completed tasks with evidence, not by how good a reply sounds.

Ask before it matters

Payments, bookings and messages to other people wait for your approval.

Your memory is yours

You can see, correct and delete what AskGogo remembers. We do not sell personal data.

Local by default

Searches, bookings and services are chosen for where you live, in rupees or dirhams, on the sites you actually use.
