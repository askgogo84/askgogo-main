Memory. Action. Continuity.

AskGogo knows what is on your plate, and does something about it.

It lives in WhatsApp, so there is nothing to install and nothing new to learn. You write to it the way you would write to a capable friend.

It keeps track of the small things that fill a day: the bill due on the fifth, the visa appointment, the receipt you will need in March. When a task needs a website, a form or a follow-up, it does that part too, and checks with you before it pays, sends or books.

How a task runs

One request, followed to the end.

Most assistants stop at an answer. AskGogo keeps going until there is a result you can check, and pauses at the one step that needs you.

Memory

Tell it once. Your preferences, people, places and documents stay with you, each with a note of where it came from. Correct anything, or make it forget.

Action

It handles the steps you would rather not: sign-ins, forms, follow-ups. Payments, bookings and messages to other people wait for your yes.

Continuity

It keeps working when you put the phone down, and the same assistant picks up on the web. A task is done only when there is proof it worked.
