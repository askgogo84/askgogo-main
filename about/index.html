About

Built in Bengaluru, for the world.

We think the next useful step for AI is not a better answer. It is a finished task.

Why we are building this

Daily life runs on OTP logins, appointment portals, utility websites and long WhatsApp threads, in Bengaluru and almost everywhere else. That is a lot of small, repetitive work. An assistant that carries it, carefully and with your permission, gives people real time back.

AskGogo is in private beta and opens to new people in small groups as the product earns it.

Founders

Goverdhan M D, Srinivas J and Mathew Varkey

Between us, we have spent our careers building consumer technology companies in India, including one that was acquired. We started AskGogo to build the assistant we wanted for ourselves: one that remembers, acts and finishes the job.

What we hold ourselves to

Four commitments.

Finish, then report

We judge ourselves by completed tasks with evidence, not by how good a reply sounds.

Ask before it matters

Payments, bookings and messages to other people wait for your approval.

Your memory is yours

You can see and correct what AskGogo remembers. We do not sell personal data.

Local by default

Searches, bookings and services are chosen for where you live, in your currency, on the sites you actually use.
