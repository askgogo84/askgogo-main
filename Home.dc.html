FAQ

Useful answers, without the fine print maze.

Do I have to use special commands?

No. AskGogo is designed for natural language. Say what you want the way you would say it to a person.

Does WhatsApp and the dashboard share the same memory?

Yes. They are two surfaces for the same AskGogo world — the same reminders, memory, lists and connected context.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Free is intended to let you experience the actual product before deciding whether Lite, Pro or Power is worth it for you.

Is there a mobile app?

The native Android and iOS apps are the next product step. Until then, WhatsApp and the web dashboard are the main surfaces.
